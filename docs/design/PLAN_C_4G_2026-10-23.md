## 1. What was built

### 1.1 Model

```
E = sum_i E_i(G_i, q_i) + 1/2 q^T E_coul q + sum_i eps_{z_i}
```

* **Local descriptors** (`plan_c_4g/descriptors.py`):
  * Radial: element-resolved Gaussian RBF sums (12 RBFs over 0.55–6.0 Å,
    width 0.45 Å, cosine cutoff), 12 × 4 = 48 features.
  * Angular: element-pair-resolved G4-like sums over neighbour pairs,
    8 basis functions (η ∈ {0.3, 1.5} Å⁻², ζ ∈ {2,4}, λ ∈ {−1,1}),
    8 × 10 pair channels = 80 features.
  * Smooth cosine cutoffs; both descriptor sets are sums of differentiable
    functions of the positions.
* **Small MLP** (`plan_c_4g/model.py`): trunk 192 → 144 (SiLU), property heads
  χ (32) and J (32, softplus + 0.5 eV), energy head 64 fed with
  `[h, q/q_scale]`. 72,087 parameters in the final checkpoint.
* **QEq** (`plan_c_4g/qeq.py`): periodic Gaussian-smeared Coulomb operator
  (σ = 0.6 Å) on a 72³ cubic B-spline grid (h = 0.75 Å for the 53.74 Å box),
  FFT reciprocal-space kernel with B-spline deconvolution, matrix-free
  preconditioned CG in the sum-zero subspace. QEq solves
  `(E_coul + diag(J)) q + χ = −μ1`, `1^T q = 0`.
* **Force trick** (`plan_c_4g/model.py`): one adjoint solve
  `A y + ν1 = −(∂E_s/∂q + E_coul q)`, then a single autograd pass through the
  surrogate

  ```
  E_surr = E_s + 1/2 q·E_coul q + y·E_coul q
           + sum_i y_i q_i J_i + sum_i y_i χ_i + sum_i eps_{z_i}
  ```

  with `q`, `y` detached. `−dE_surr/dR` is the exact force; the same surrogate
  gives the exact parameter gradient by the envelope theorem. The reported
  energy uses the physical `E_pred`, with a straight-through value for the
  energy loss (`E_graph_train`).
* **Neighbours** (`plan_c_4g/neighbors.py`): periodic cell list, padded
  neighbour table, exact minimum-image shifts; a custom autograd Function for
  the distance/displacement backward (see §4.2).

### 1.2 Data and training

* Gas labels: `bench/path_b_data2_q4_2026-10-22/attempt-01/data/{train,valid}.xyz`
  (1,734 / 432), six molecules (H2O, NH3, C2H4, CH4, C2H6, CO2), 16 Å boxes,
  FP64 PolarMACE teacher labels.
* Bulk labels: `bench/path_b_bulk_q4_2026-10-23/attempt-01/data/*.xyz`
  (pilot: 32 molecules/box, densities 1e-4–0.1 molecules/Å³, 150–2000 K;
  6 species; 198/174/132/… frames per species; 65 held-out states).
* Bulk2: `bench/path_b_bulk2_c_2026-10-23/attempt-01/data/C2H4_bulk.xyz`
  appeared during this segment (3,300 frames, 33 states, densities 1e-4–0.01
  at the time of writing); used only as a held-out set, not for training.
* Teacher model: `task3_sampling_database/mlip/models/mace-polar/MACE-POLAR-1-M.model`
  (loads with the local MACE stack when `PYTHONPATH` includes
  `external-repos/graph_electrostatics-0.4.0`; not needed at student runtime).
* Training: AdamW + cosine schedule, batch 32 for gas-only and 8 for bulk,
  energy loss = per-atom MSE, force loss = per-component MSE with weight 20–50,
  gradient clipping; checkpoints by held-out error.

### 2.2 Measured speed (µs/atom/step)

| Configuration | µs/atom/step | CG iterations | CG residual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| FP64, full E+F, CG max 120 (converged) | **19.26** | 88 | 6.6e-10 |
| FP64, full E+F, CG max 60 | **13.45** | 60 | 8.8e-7 |
| FP64, energy only, CG max 60 | 8.48 | 60 | 8.8e-7 |
| FP32, full E+F, CG max 60 | 15.10 | 60 | 5.1e-6 |
| FP32, energy only, CG max 60 | 7.50 | 60 | 5.1e-6 |
| FP64, training step (forward+backward+force loss) | 30.16 | 88 | 6.6e-10 |
| FP64, training step (CG max 60) | 31.77 | 60 | 8.8e-7 |
| Neighbour-list rebuild (both cutoffs), per rebuild | 2.36 | — | — |
| Descriptors (forward, no graph) | 0.44 | — | — |

At 60 CG iterations the QEq is not fully converged (8.8e-7); the force-trick
consistency is still ~1e-6 eV/Å, far below the 30 meV/Å gate, so this is a
legitimate operating point for MD, not an accuracy shortcut. The fully
converged number (19.26) is the honest headline for an accuracy-gated run.

### 2.3 Component microbenchmarks

| Component | µs/atom/step | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| Neighbour rebuild (radial+angular) | 2.36 | rebuilt once; amortised in MD (every 10–20 steps => 0.12–0.24) |
| Descriptor forward | 0.44 | real G2/G4 evaluation, no graph |
| PME stencil build | 0.156 | once per forward; the graph copy is a second build |
| PME apply (scatter+FFT+gather) | 0.040 | one matvec |
| FFT pair (rfftn+irfftn) | 0.015 | 72³ FP64 |
| QEq forward solve, 60 iterations | 5.30 | 41% of the 13.45 µs full EF |
| QEq forward solve, 80 iterations (4.6e-9) | 9.42 | converged solve alone |
| QEq adjoint solve, 88 iterations (2.3e-9) | 10.64 | converged adjoint alone |
| Full E+F at 60 iterations | 13.45 | two QEq solves + backward + force assembly |
| Peak GPU memory | 1.06 GB | FP64; 0.55 GB FP32 |

The QEq solve dominates: the converged forward and adjoint solves alone measure
**9.42 + 10.64 = 20.1 µs/atom/step**, i.e. essentially the whole converged
19.26 µs/atom/step budget (the two measurements are separate runs; the
difference is GPU timing variance). At the looser 60-iteration setting the
forward solve is 5.30 µs/atom and the full EF is 13.45. The next speed lever
is therefore the linear solve, not the descriptors or the MLP.

### 2.4 What the earlier fake benchmark missed

The previous 0.72 µs/atom prototype used random descriptors, a K=30
aggregation, a 64-wide MLP and a dense Coulomb matrix with 20 CG iterations,
and did not implement the force trick. The realistic prototype adds:

* real neighbour lists and G2/G4 evaluation (0.44 µs/atom),
* the FFT/PME stencil and two QEq solves (10.6 µs/atom at 60 iterations),
* the force-trick adjoint and the autograd backward through descriptors
  (~2–4 µs/atom),
* the periodic box and the graph-based force assembly.

The measured 13.45–19.26 µs/atom/step is the number that should replace the
0.72 projection.

### 2.5 Negative results and fixes found on the way

1. **`index_put` backward through `pos[nbr]`** cost 7.3–14.5 µs/atom in the
   force path (FP64 atomics with many duplicate indices). Replaced by a custom
   geometry Function with two `index_add` calls; the force path dropped from
   95.9 to 59.5 µs/atom in the 42k model.
2. **`eps[zi]` indexing** (5,184 atomics into 4 parameter addresses) cost
   several µs/atom. Replaced by `onehot @ eps`.
3. **Python stencil construction** (a 64-iteration loop + 64 tensor stacks)
   cost 1.18 µs/atom per PME apply. Vectorised and cached: the apply is
   0.040 µs/atom, the build 0.156 µs/atom.
4. **Recursive CG residual drift / divergence** after convergence in FP64.
   Added residual replacement every 8 iterations and projected the
   preconditioned direction onto the sum-zero subspace; the QEq solution now
   matches a dense solve to 4e-17.
5. **A k-space preconditioner** (inverse of the translation-invariant
   `E_coul + mean(J)`) was implemented and measured; it did **not** improve
   the condition number of the projected operator (≈7 vs 2.6 for the raw
   operator) and was removed from the production path. The Jacobi diagonal
   (J + 1/(σ√π)) is used instead.
6. **FP32 is not faster** for the 72k model (15.10 vs 13.45 µs/atom at the same
   60-iteration cap), and FP32 CG stalls at 5e-6. Mixed precision is not the
   lever here; launch/atomics/solve dominate. The 42k model did show a small
   FP32 gain (10.27 vs 11.47), so the conclusion is model-size dependent.

## 3. Training prototype and accuracy

### 3.1 Protocol

* Two runs:
  1. **Gas-only** (`results/plan_c_4g_gas160.pt`): hidden 160, 42,023
     parameters, 1,734 gas frames, 120 epochs, batch 32, lr 2e-3 cosine,
     force weight 50, 20 CG iterations. Wall 6.5 min.
  2. **Combined gas+bulk** (`results/plan_c_4g_combo120.pt`): hidden 192,
     72,087 parameters, 120 epochs, batch 8; gas batch (16 frames) every 4th
     step; bulk pilot 5 species (C2H4, C2H6, CH4, H2O, NH3; CO2_bulk.xyz
     appeared after the run started); lr 1e-3 cosine, force weight 20,
     40 CG iterations. Wall 12.2 min. Best checkpoint at step 9,020 by bulk
     energy.
* Held-out protocol:
  * gas: the provided 432-frame valid split;
  * bulk pilot: deterministic state-level 20% holdout
    (species, density, temperature), 195 frames / 65 states, reproduced from
    the training split seed;
  * bulk2 C2H4: 33 stratified states (one frame per state; densities
    1e-4–0.01, temperatures 150–2000 K), **never used in training**;
  * six-molecule smoke: 6 frames, 12 molecules/box, OOD.

### 3.4 Honest gate statement

No model in this segment passes 5 meV/atom. The force gate is within 1.1× for
the gas-only model and within 1.3–3.9× for the combined model. The best
evidence that the architecture can approach the force gate is:

* gas-only: 32.6 meV/Å aggregate, with NH3 8.4, H2O 9.0, CH4 14.1,
  C2H6 17.7, C2H4 32.1, CO2 160.2;
* six-molecule smoke: 29.1 meV/Å aggregate;
* bulk pilot: 95.0 meV/Å, with H2O 54.7, CH4 24.6, C2H4 73.8, C2H6 66.7,
  NH3 104.1, CO2 372.5 (untrained bulk).

The energy error is the hard blocker. C2H4/C2H6 are systematically 84–96
meV/atom even in gas, so the local descriptor/MLP cannot represent the
hydrocarbon binding to the required accuracy.

## 4. Why the gas-only bulk evaluation failed (and the fix)

The first bulk evaluation of the gas-only checkpoint gave **22.5 eV/atom**
(H2O pilot). Diagnosis on single frames:

| Density (molecules/Å³) | Neighbours < 6 Å | max standardized feature | E_s / atom (eV) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1e-4 | 2.0 | 2.6 | −0.10 |
| 5e-4 | 2.0 | 2.4 | −0.10 |
| 2e-3 | 5.9 | **72,206** | −9.35 |
| 5e-3 | 15.7 | **214,569** | −70.0 |
| 1e-2 | 24.5 | **416,428** | −45.2 |

The energy blow-up was **not** the electrostatics (E_elec stayed ~0.1–0.3 eV);
it was the local MLP seeing inputs scaled by 1e5. The gas-only
`FeatureScaler` divided by per-feature standard deviations that were
numerically zero for features absent in gas (e.g. O–O radial/angular channels
in a single H2O molecule). At bulk density those features become finite and
were amplified by 1e5.

Fix (implemented):

1. `FeatureScaler.fit` now uses an RMS-based floor: features with
   RMS < 1e-2 get unit scale, and all scales are clamped at 1e-2. This removes
   the 1e5 amplification.
2. The combined model fits the scaler on a stratified gas+bulk sample, so
   density-induced feature ranges are in-distribution.
3. Bulk batches are formed only inside a cell group, and the bulk holdout is
   split at the (species, density, temperature) state level.

After the fix, the combined model's bulk error is 73 meV/atom (pilot) and
95 meV/atom (bulk2 C2H4), i.e. a factor of ~300 better than the broken
extrapolation. This is the most important engineering result of the segment:
**a per-feature standardization fit only on gas is unsafe for bulk.**

## 5. Concrete training design for the 43k bulk2 corpus

The 43k bulk2 campaign (`t-20261023100114-9pdj8`, `t-20261023100130-kvvrg`,
`t-20261023100147-8bq5c`) is the right next step. At the time of writing only
`C2H4_bulk.xyz` (3,300 frames / 33 states) had appeared; the other files were
still running.

### 5.1 Data

* Use all six species; 32 molecules/box; densities 1e-4–0.1 molecules/Å³;
  150–2000 K; 100 frames/state (bulk2) vs 3 frames/state (pilot).
* Split **by state** (species, density, temperature), not by frame: 80/10/10
  train/valid/test. Keep the gas 432-frame valid set as an additional
  in-distribution test.
* Reject/downweight states with teacher F max > 25 eV/Å (the same filter as
  the generator) and keep a separate "extreme" test bucket.
* Fit the scaler on a stratified sample of gas + all bulk densities; keep the
  RMS floor from §4.

### 5.2 Model and training

* Same 4G architecture, but scale to hidden 256 / trunk 192 / heads 128
  (~130k parameters) and add descriptor resolution: 16 RBFs (width 0.35 Å)
  and 12–16 angular basis functions (η up to 6 Å⁻²) to resolve C–C/C=C.
* Batch 8–16 frames per cell group; mix gas at the **atom** level (gas frames
  are 3–8 atoms, bulk 96–192): target 20–30% of atoms from gas/rattle data.
* Loss: per-atom energy MSE + `w_F` per-component force MSE. Start `w_F = 20`;
  sweep 10/20/50. Use gradient clipping 1.0 and a 5-epoch linear warm-up.
* Two stages:
  1. bulk-dominant (gas every 4th batch) for 120–200 epochs;
  2. gas+rattle fine-tune (gas every 2nd batch, `w_F = 50`, lr 2e-4) for
     30–40 epochs, selecting by gas valid E and checking bulk held-out.
* Teacher cost: 43k frames at 0.1–0.5 s/frame is 1.2–6 h of A100 time; the
  student training at ~30 µs/atom/step is not the bottleneck.

### 5.3 Accuracy levers, in order

1. **Hydrocarbon energy bias.** Add finer radial resolution around 1.2–1.6 Å
   and more angular channels; test a small per-element-pair or
   coordination-dependent reference. This is the dominant error (C2H4/C2H6
   84–96 meV/atom in gas).
2. **CO2 forces.** CO2 is 160 meV/Å in gas and 372 meV/Å in bulk (no bulk
   training). Add CO2 bulk data and a dedicated stiff-mode check.
3. **Force gate.** The gas-only model is already at 32.6 meV/Å; a larger model
   + force-weight sweep + longer schedule is the shortest path to 30.
4. **QEq convergence / speed.** The QEq solve is 41–80% of the runtime. A
   better preconditioner, a direct solve for small boxes, or a fixed-iteration
   contract would buy speed. Do not change the operator without re-training.

### 5.4 What not to do

* Do not refit a gas-only scaler on bulk and then reuse the gas checkpoint
  without fine-tuning: the feature rescaling shifts every input (§4).
* Do not select checkpoints by bulk energy alone: the epoch-39 checkpoint had
  bulk E 59.8 / F 137.6 while epoch-109 had 33.7 / 65.4; force and energy
  improve at different rates.
* Do not quote the 0.72 µs/atom random-descriptor number as a Plan C result.

## 6. Limitations of this segment

* The PME operator is a Gaussian-smeared grid Coulomb, not exact GTO-Ewald.
  It is symmetric by construction and was validated against a direct k-sum and
  finite differences; it is not yet validated against the exact engine's
  Ewald. The student is trained with the same operator, so this is a model
  choice, not an inference inconsistency.
* No stress/virial and no cell gradients; the prototype is energy/force only.
* The CG is matrix-free but not quasi-linear in the number of iterations for
  large boxes; the 5,184-atom solve needs 88 iterations at 1e-9.
* The bulk pilot is small (195 held-out frames, 65 states) and the bulk2
  corpus was incomplete. The bulk numbers are preliminary.
* The 43k bulk2 data is not used for training in this segment.
